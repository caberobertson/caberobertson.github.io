About Me

§ About

I am an Electrical Engineering student concentrating in Communications & Signal Processing at New Mexico State University (expected Dec 2027). My work centers on building real-time embedded systems that turn noisy sensor data into reliable device actions — sensor-fusion firmware, BLE device links, and decoding pipelines — across three engineering internships and a signal-processing research assistantship.

That has meant fielding an RF communication-jamming demonstrator for defense clients at Syndetix, characterizing BLDC motor drive and running a design of experiments at GILZ, validating bit-error-rate performance for channel decoding in the Mitchell Coding Group, and currently standing up an unmanned systems production floor as the sole engineer at ETV America. I am seeking a Summer 2027 internship in RF, embedded, and mission systems hardware.

I also serve as Vice President of the IEEE NMSU Student Chapter, where I tripled active membership from 10 to 30+, lead a $50,000 fundraising initiative for the ECE department, and act as primary liaison to industry sponsors and national IEEE.
